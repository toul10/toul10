## Public projects

### Safe, auditable AI

- **[agent-authority-pilot](https://github.com/toul10/agent-authority-pilot):** does an AI agent tell apart having access, being asked and being authorised? 65 scenarios and three safeguards (prompt policy, code checks, a monitor model), run on Claude, GPT, Gemini or Grok, with a local app to read every conversation. Results: [published runs](https://toul10.github.io/agent-authority-pilot/). Python, MIT.
- **[mailme](https://github.com/toul10/mailme):** MailMe, a self-hosted assistant that turns a mailbox into a short task list: what to do, what you are waiting for and what you promised. The AI only proposes changes, each with the sentence it read, and deterministic code decides what is applied. Works with Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or Grok; extraction quality was measured with Claude. TypeScript and PostgreSQL, MIT.

### Runs on your own machine

- **[privatevault](https://github.com/toul10/privatevault):** PrivateVault, an offline, zero-knowledge secrets manager for macOS. The key that opens the vault needs two secrets, a Master Password and a separate Secret Key that never sits in the vault file, and the app has no network access. The security architecture, the threat model and a self-audit are in the repository. Swift, source-available under FSL-1.1-ALv2.
- **[aisocial](https://github.com/toul10/aisocial):** AI Social, a small local app that writes one version of a post for each platform (X, LinkedIn, a Facebook Page) and publishes it now or on a schedule, after you have reviewed it. English and Greek. Python, MIT.

### Products and developer tools

- **[wedding-planner](https://github.com/toul10/wedding-planner):** MyWedTime, a wedding planner that began as a hosted product and is now free and open source: guests and RSVPs, seating, budget, vendors, the wedding-day timeline and a wedding website, in English and Greek. Next.js and PostgreSQL, self-hosted with Docker, MIT.
- **[inspwal](https://github.com/toul10/inspwal):** an inspector for Walrus blobs and Sui objects that shows reachability, hashes, Seal encryption status and the related Sui records. Node.js, ISC.
